Combine King Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Combine King ("the game", package name com.inia.combineking) is developed by inia ("we", "us") and distributed on Google Play.

Contact: inia.devel@gmail.com

1. Summary

Combine King is a harvesting game. It shows no ads and has no account system. From version 1.0.0 it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase, so we can see how the game is played and fix what breaks (Section 4). Your farm itself stays on your phone.

2. Permissions

The game uses internet access, only to send the statistics and crash reports described in Section 4. It asks for no other permissions and does not use your advertising ID.

3. Data stored on your device

Your farm — the wheat in your barn, your money, the machines you own and the fields you have left open — is saved in the game's private storage on your phone, so you can carry on where you left off. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The wheat price in the game's market is worked out on your phone from the time of day; the game does not fetch it from anywhere.

4. Usage statistics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played. It reports:

  • that the game was installed, opened, updated or removed, and how long it was played;
  • what happens in the game: which field size, shape and machine a field was played with, how much of it was harvested and for how long, when the autonomous mode was used, and when combines or wheat were bought or sold (game money only);
  • the game version, Android version, device model, language and approximate country.

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to learn when the game crashes. A crash report contains what the game was doing when it crashed (a technical stack trace), the game and Android version and the device model.

Both are attached to a random identifier Firebase creates for the installation, not to your name, email or advertising ID. The game does not collect your advertising ID and sends no signals for ad personalisation. We look at this data only in aggregate — how many people play, how often, for how long, what they play — and never use it to identify or contact anyone. Google processes it under the Firebase privacy and security terms; per-user analytics data is retained for up to 14 months.

The game shows no advertisements and has no servers of our own.

5. Google Play

The game is installed and updated through Google Play. Google may collect information as part of providing that service, for example to check that the game was installed from Google Play. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us. If in-app purchases are added in a future version, they will be handled entirely by Google Play, and we will never see your payment details; this page will be updated first.

6. Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If what the game collects ever changes, this page changes first, and the effective date above with it.

8. Contact

Questions may be directed to inia.devel@gmail.com.
