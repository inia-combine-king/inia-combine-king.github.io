Combine King Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Combine King ("the game", package name com.inia.combineking) is developed by inia ("we", "us") and distributed on Google Play.

Contact: inia.devel@gmail.com

1. Summary

Combine King is a harvesting game that runs entirely on your device. It collects no personal data, shows no ads, has no account system and does not use the internet. We never see anything you do in the game.

2. Permissions

The game asks for no permissions. It has no internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

3. Data stored on your device

Your farm — the wheat in your barn, your money and the machines you own — is saved in the game's private storage on your phone, so you can carry on where you left off. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The wheat price in the game's market is worked out on your phone from the time of day; the game does not fetch it from anywhere.

4. Advertising and analytics

The game shows no advertisements and contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software. It does not use your advertising ID.

5. Google Play

The game is installed and updated through Google Play. Google may collect information as part of providing that service, for example to check that the game was installed from Google Play. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us. If in-app purchases are added in a future version, they will be handled entirely by Google Play, and we will never see your payment details; this page will be updated first.

6. Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, children included.

7. Changes to this policy

If what the game collects ever changes, this page changes first, and the effective date above with it.

8. Contact

Questions may be directed to inia.devel@gmail.com.
